Downrush is a polyphonic synth for standalone Akai MPCs and the Force. It runs the synth engine of the open-source Deluge community firmware (v1.3.0) and plays Deluge SYNTH preset files with the firmware's own signal processing: subtractive, FM and ring mod, sample oscillators, a 24-cable mod matrix, arpeggiator, the firmware's effects and reverb.

**This is a preview.** The list of parameters may still change, and a project saved with it may not reload correctly in a later version. A plugin runs inside the MPC software: save your work often.

Unofficial: not made, endorsed or supported by Synthstrom Audible or by inMusic / Akai.

## What you need
- A standalone MPC or a Force on MPC OS 3.10.1 or later, with Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) > Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support switched on.
- Tested on one Akai Force (32-bit). The package also holds a build for the 64-bit MPCs, which has not yet been run on one.

## Install
Download `Downrush-0.1.0-mpc-sdk.zip`, unzip it, and copy the folder `Jamie Fenton - VST - Downrush` into a folder named `VST2 Plugins` at the top level of a drive the MPC can see. `INSTALL.txt` in the zip has the steps in full.

## Sounds
Twelve starter presets come with it. Deluge factory presets and preset collections are not included: they belong to their makers. If you own a Deluge, `README.md` in the zip says how to bring your own synths and their samples over:
https://github.com/jamiefaye/mpc-deluge/blob/v0.1.0/plugins/deluge-synth/release/README.md#bringing-your-deluge-synths-over
